## Workflow automation is having a moment. Here is what TechFlow actually sells.

TechFlow started in 2019. The product is task management with an analytics layer on top,
sold to mid-market ops teams.

Three things are worth knowing. Permissions are set per person rather than per role, which
matters if you have contractors. It connects to the tools you already run, though the list
of supported integrations is shorter than the marketing page implies and you should check
yours before committing. And the analytics are read-only, so you cannot trigger anything
from a dashboard.

The company is growing. I do not have a number, and neither does anyone outside the
company, so treat "rapid growth" as a claim rather than a fact until they publish one.

The competitive question is the real one. Every large suite ships some version of this now,
usually worse but usually free with something the buyer already pays for. That is the thing
to ask them about.
